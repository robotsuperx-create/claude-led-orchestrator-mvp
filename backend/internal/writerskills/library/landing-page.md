name: landing-page
description: Marketing, product or company sites. A page structure that carries the visitor from first look to action.
---
A strong default order, adapted to the brief:
1. Hero: the promise, a supporting line, the primary action, and a striking visual.
2. Proof right after: who it is for, logos or numbers that are real, or a short demonstration.
3. Benefits in the reader's terms, three to five, each with a heading and a sentence, laid out with variety (not only identical cards).
4. How it works or the product up close: steps, a showcase, details that build desire.
5. Story or values when the brand matters (heritage, craft, people).
6. Social proof only if real content was given.
7. Offer or pricing when relevant, clear and comparable.
8. Frequently asked questions that remove the last objections.
9. Final call to action, then a complete footer (navigation, contact, legal links).

- The navigation is short (four to six links) and stays reachable.
- Each section has one job and a distinct layout so the page has rhythm.
- The page must work as one coherent piece: the same tokens, the same voice, the same visual idea from top to bottom.
