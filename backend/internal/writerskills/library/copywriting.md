---
name: copywriting
description: Any page with words on it. Real, specific content in the brand's voice instead of placeholders.
---
- Write for one reader with one goal. The headline says what they get, in their words, in under ten words. The line under it says how or why to believe it.
- Specific beats grand: numbers, materials, places, times, names of real product features. Cut adjectives that any brand could claim.
- Every section answers a question the reader has at that point (what is it, why trust it, what does it cost, what do I do next).
- One primary call to action, repeated at natural decision points, with a verb that names the outcome ("Start my order", not "Submit").
- Match the language and tone of the task. In Arabic, write natural, modern phrasing, not a translation.
- No lorem ipsum, no "Feature 1", no invented testimonials or statistics presented as real. If a fact is unknown, write copy that does not need it.
