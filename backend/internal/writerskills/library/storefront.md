---
name: storefront
description: Shops, product pages, carts and checkouts. Pages that build trust and make buying easy.
---
- Product page: large imagery first, then name, price, key variant choices, one clear add-to-cart, delivery and returns in one line each, then details, materials, sizing and care.
- Prices are unmistakable, with currency; sale prices show the original crossed out.
- Trust near the decision: delivery time, returns policy, secure payment, real contact details.
- Collections: consistent product cards (image, name, price, one short line), filters that fit the catalogue, and no layout shift when images load.
- Cart and checkout: as few steps and fields as possible, totals always visible, errors next to the field that caused them.
- Never invent reviews, ratings or stock levels. Leave clearly marked empty states if data is missing.
